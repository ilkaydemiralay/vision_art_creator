# Scene 01 — The unopened letter
INT. SMALL ROOM — LATE AFTERNOON
Deniz enters the edge of the room. A closed envelope rests on the table. Deniz notices it, takes two measured steps toward it, and stops. Looking down, Deniz exhales. The letter stays untouched. Cut.
No dialogue, no visible writing, no second character. Total 15 seconds. Envelope color is delegated to production design.
