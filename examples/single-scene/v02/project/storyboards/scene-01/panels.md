# Storyboard — scene 01
Text panels; images not generated.
Deniz, a 35-year-old adult, short dark hair, plain plum wool cardigan, charcoal trousers, no accessories.
A small quiet room; off-white matte wall, one oak table, window on frame left, closed door on frame right.
One closed cream paper envelope lies flat at the center of the oak table; no legible writing.
16:9. Soft late-afternoon window light from frame left. Muted natural color. Camera stays on the south side of the table; Deniz moves screen left to right. No axis crossing.
01: 0–5 s, locked wide 35 mm, Deniz at left notices the envelope on the table at right.
02: 5–10 s, medium 50 mm from same axis, Deniz takes two slow steps left to right and stops at the table.
03: 10–15 s, medium close 65 mm on face and upper torso, eyeline down-right; shoulders settle, no touch. Cut before any reach.
Panel 01: negative space between Deniz and table makes distance legible. Panel 02: two steps close that distance; retain the table at right. Panel 03: held eyeline and shoulder release show refusal without revealing the letter. No reverse angle. Envelope remains fixed in world space even when outside frame.
