# Room and envelope — scene 01
A small quiet room; off-white matte wall, one oak table, window on frame left, closed door on frame right.
One closed red paper envelope lies flat at the center of the oak table; no legible writing.
The envelope never moves; no additional papers, furniture or decoration. The window is the only motivated light source. The off-white wall separates the plum cardigan without changing the director’s restrained palette.
